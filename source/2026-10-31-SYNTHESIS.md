# Council round, Oct 31, 2026: synthesis and decisions

Eight reviewers read the full set (general plan with appendices, board brief, technical brief, Christian edition, both charters, Budget v3): a nonprofit executive, a business-plan professor, a serial founder, a board-governance consultant, an AI-evaluation researcher, a program officer, an interfaith scholar and a nonprofit CFO. Their reviews are in this folder, dated 2026-10-31. This note records what the next version applies, what it applies in modified form, what it declines, and what only the founder can settle.

Three rules governed the synthesis. Decisions Jimmy Lin made on Oct 27–28 (fractional ED at $60K in 2027; two revenue paths with the given numbers; no founder pay; engineer and fellow first; three-month reserve; the v1 counts) stand. Verified errors are corrected even where they touch an adopted number, and the correction is flagged below. Everything that needs a fact only the founder has goes to the question list at the end and to the internal checklist, and the circulated documents no longer carry "[name]", "to confirm" or "hours to confirm" placeholders.

## A. Applied across all documents

1. **One clock, one tier table.** Months count from the first paid hire. The tiers (verbatim in plan, brief and workbook): under $60K received by Mar 31, 2027: all-volunteer, a two-module preprint with the say–do and sycophancy pilots. $60K–$120K: engineer contracted, president as interim leader, two-module preprint, v1 in 2028 (the Conservative path). $120K–$200K: engineer and technical co-lead, four modules on the development split, no test split or profile reports. $200K or more: full v1 within 12 months of the first paid hire; public release contingent on Tranche 2 by mid-2027. The second 18-month clock is dropped: the one stop rule is "no one outside CEI has run the suite within 18 months of v1's release, so no second version on the same distribution model."
2. **v1 core and full v1.** Tranche 1 guarantees the core: two modules (one secular, one faith) plus the say–do pilot and the sycophancy probe. Tranche 2 adds the other two modules and the public release. The fifth module and the local-run kit are "stretch" everywhere. Moral psychology is labeled exploratory (no developmental-stage claims) everywhere.
3. **Hiring order, everywhere the same:** engineer (contracted first), then the technical co-lead, then the fractional Executive Director once nine months of the role's loaded cost has been received, then the fellow with Tranche 2. The nine-months rule is defined once: "No paid role, employee or contractor, starts until nine months of its fully loaded cost has been received into the sponsor's account for CEI; every 2027 role is an initial nine-to-twelve-month term, renewable on funding, and candidates are told so."
4. **The ED design is decided, not open.** The "board will choose between" sentence goes; the design is a fractional ED (about 60% time) employed through the sponsor, full time from 2028. The 90-day priority becomes "design the role, appoint a search committee of independent directors, build the comparables file; post only when nine months of the loaded cost is received." Pay figures are planning assumptions set each year by the independent directors, founders absent, against three Bay Area comparables, minuted within 60 days, with the sponsor's approval while sponsored.
5. **Employees, not contractors.** The sponsor's handbook says project directors cannot be contractors and core work is employee work; California's ABC test points the same way. The ED and engineer are sponsor employees in 2027 with a 13.5% payroll-tax load (no benefits for part-time roles); 25% with benefits from 2028. The co-lead is an advisory engagement with defined deliverables, approved by the sponsor. This corrects an adopted assumption (0% in 2027) and costs about $15K in 2027; the 2027 table is rebalanced to the $308K ask.
6. **"Endorse" becomes "ratify" (reference points) and "recognize as faithful" (modules).** Every document.
7. **Numbers made consistent:** about $308K (not $300K); a 30-minute conversation; seven to nine directors with an independent majority; "$7K+ rapid-response sub-award through Faith Work & Tech's Notre Dame DELTA grant"; "60% of Protestant pastors"; the technical co-lead in every hires list; the fellow at $38K in 2027 (mid-year start, $75K a year); "a part-time contract engineer (about 500 hours)" in place of "half-time".
8. **Placeholders out of circulated text.** "[name], [N] hours a week", "(proposed; hours to confirm)", "to be confirmed", "(to be named only with its agreement)" are removed; the facts behind them go to the internal checklist and the question list.
9. **Dates.** Every document carries Oct 31, 2026 as its revision date.

## C. Technical brief

- Say–do operationalized: goal from an operator system prompt; a three-level cost ladder (inconvenience; task failure; task failure plus an explicit operator penalty); the action read deterministically from tool calls; the "say" judgment elicited with the full trade-off under three framings before the agentic run; intervals clustered by template; a pre-registered minimum detectable gap; "a fidelity failure is not a specification violation; operator-following is reported as a covariate." Claim-level validation; refreshed at each rotation and for each new model family; the refusal classifier reports its own agreement.
- Item drafting disclosed: the drafting model family recorded per item; a second sensitivity row with the drafting family's judge removed; a cap on the AI-drafted share; scholars rewrite, not approve.
- Capability correlation: a named composite index and a pre-registered threshold in the registration; frontier profiles also shown as residuals; the sweep dated against the dev-split release and frozen at v1; test items drawn from the same templates and strata as dev.
