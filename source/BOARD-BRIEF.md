# CEI in two pages: a brief for board candidates

## The thesis, in three sentences

AI will be aligned to something, and today which values the most powerful models are trained toward is decided mostly inside a few companies. The Center for Ethical Intelligence (CEI) translates the world's moral traditions, religious and secular, into open benchmarks and evaluations that test how faithfully models represent those traditions and whether they act on the values they state. We are a research organization: our success is rigorous, open, published work; use by labs and auditors is influence we track, not the bar we are judged by.

## What CEI builds

- **Four benchmark modules in development,** each ratified by scholars of its source: normative ethics; moral psychology (exploratory); the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights; and Pope Leo XIV's 2026 encyclical *Magnifica humanitas* (in v1; CEI's draft is a pilot until the Catholic panel we are recruiting ratifies it). Tranche 1 guarantees the v1 core: two modules, one secular and one faith, plus the say–do pilot and the sycophancy probe; Tranche 2 adds the other two and the public release; a fifth module and a local-run kit are stretch.
- **Five tasks:** representation, application, reasoning quality, say–do (acting on a stated value when it costs the goal) and sycophancy (verdicts shifting with the user's stated identity).
- **Per-model profile reports,** no composite score, badge or certification; labs see results under embargo and cannot edit them.
- **An open method** (templates, rubric guides, validation protocol, code) for other traditions to author their own modules. AI Alignment is the 2027 flagship; AI Governance and the forming Scholar Group run on volunteer time.

## Proof so far (all volunteer, about $7K awarded)

- **Governance Symphony:** a 5,140-statement corpus of AI-governance statements from 130 countries, about 2,700 coded so far by two AI models into six clusters of principles (human validation in 2027); co-authored with researchers at Notre Dame and Harvard. One early finding: solidarity and religious-pastoral principles appear only when faith, labor and Indigenous sources are in the data.
- **Relationships and publications:** a $7K+ rapid-response sub-award through Faith Work & Tech's Notre Dame DELTA grant; an invited participant in Anthropic's March 2026 summit of Christian leaders; a Praxis 2027 Nonprofit Accelerator fellow; one conference abstract (5ICEAI; ICAIR 2026). No CEI paper is yet published or preprinted.

## The 12-month plan (months count from the first paid hire)

**Before the first hire (by Q1 2027):** an exploratory volunteer pilot of the sycophancy, reasoning-quality and say–do tasks on three open models; the chair and at least three independent directors seated at one meeting; the methods committee chartered.

1. **Months 1–3:** engineer, then technical co-lead; harness built; core-module scholar panels seated.
2. **Months 4–8:** reference answers ratified; AI judges validated against them; the say–do pilot (about 60 scenarios) pre-registered and run.
3. **Months 9–12:** at least six frontier and open models scored; v1 published with datasheet and profile reports; preprint submitted to NeurIPS 2028 Evaluations & Datasets.

**Funding tiers:** under $60K received by Mar 31, 2027: all-volunteer, a two-module preprint with the say–do and sycophancy pilots. $60K–$120K: engineer contracted, president as interim leader, two-module preprint, v1 in 2028 (the Conservative path). $120K–$210K: engineer and technical co-lead, four modules on the development split, no test split or profile reports. $210K or more: full v1 within 12 months of the first paid hire; public release contingent on Tranche 2 by mid-2027.

**Hiring rule and order.** No paid role, employee or contractor, starts until nine months of its fully loaded cost has been received into the sponsor's account for CEI; every 2027 role is an initial nine-to-twelve-month term, renewable on funding, and candidates are told so. Order: engineer, then technical co-lead, then the fractional Executive Director once nine months of its loaded cost is received, then the fellow with Tranche 2.

## Governance

- **Entity.** CEI is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation (work began in 2024; Articles approved 2026), run as a fiscally sponsored project of Social Good Fund under a Model C (pre-approved grant) agreement at 6.5% of receipts. The sponsor receives all funds and grants for CEI, employs the 2027 staff, holds title to data and code until released under open licenses, charges a fee on receipts (planned at 6%) and has final approval of legal, financial and employment matters; CEI's directors govern the corporation and set program, policy, budget requests and the leader's hiring subject to that approval. We plan to file Form 1023 in Q1 2028 and move funds, staff and data to CEI's own 501(c)(3) in mid-2029, when the board takes full control and the fee ends.
- **Board:** no faith requirement for any seat; the founders are Christian and say so. Seven to nine directors with an independent majority before any salaried hire; quorum a majority; terms and removal as below; finance-and-audit and governance committees; an executive session of independent directors at every meeting.
- **Methods committee:** independent and board-chartered: secular philosophers, ML and measurement researchers and scholars of religious traditions, at least one from outside Christianity and a majority from outside it. It decides which traditions are included and how modules are scored; the board sets a decision aside only by two-thirds, after 30 days' notice and with published reasons, for a legal, contractual or budget bar.
- **Independence (adopted as the planning basis; the board ratifies):** no more than 15% of revenue from any AI company (in-kind at fair value included) and 40% from industry in total; no funder reviews results; no badges or certifications; AI-company gifts reviewed at any size; no director may work for, lead or hold a more-than-nominal stake in a company whose models CEI evaluates.

## What you are committing to

- **Time:** four board meetings a year (two in person), one committee, and five to eight hours a month in the first year.
- **Money:** a personal gift each year, meaningful to you, no minimum; as a board we aim to give or open doors to about $50K in 2027.
- **Term:** a one-year first term, then three-year terms in three classes, at most two consecutive; removal only by two-thirds of the directors in office.
- **Liability:** before any outside director joins, bylaws indemnifying directors to the full extent California allows, and D&O insurance of at least $1M.
- **Faith:** none required. The founders are Christian and say so; the board and its committees carry no confessional test.
- **Today's board:** three directors, none independent, no chair, no committees; bylaws and insurance to be adopted before you join.

You can expect no surprises and quarterly reports with tier status at the top; we want a board that holds us to what we publish, not what we intend. **Next step:** a 30-minute conversation with the founders, then the full business plan, the technical brief and the two charters.
